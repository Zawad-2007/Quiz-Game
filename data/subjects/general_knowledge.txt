# Multiple-Choice Questions for General Knowledge

Q: What is the capital city of Australia?
A: Sydney
B: Melbourne
C: Canberra
D: Brisbane
Ans: C
Exp: Canberra was chosen as the compromise capital between Sydney and Melbourne in 1908.
---
Q: Who wrote the classic play 'Romeo and Juliet'?
A: Charles Dickens
B: William Shakespeare
C: Mark Twain
D: Jane Austen
Ans: B
Exp: William Shakespeare wrote Romeo and Juliet early in his career.
---
Q: What is the largest ocean on Earth?
A: Atlantic Ocean
B: Indian Ocean
C: Arctic Ocean
D: Pacific Ocean
Ans: D
Exp: The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth.
---
Q: In which year did the Apollo 11 mission successfully land humans on the Moon?
A: 1965
B: 1969
C: 1972
D: 1975
Ans: B
Exp: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969.
---
Q: Which country is known as the Land of the Rising Sun?
A: China
B: Japan
C: South Korea
D: Thailand
Ans: B
Exp: Japan is historically called the Land of the Rising Sun (Nihon / Nippon).
---
Q: How many continents are there on Earth?
A: 5
B: 6
C: 7
D: 8
Ans: C
Exp: The seven continents are Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, and Australia.
