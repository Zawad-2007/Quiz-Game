# Multiple-Choice Questions for Science

Q: What is the chemical symbol for Gold?
A: Ag
B: Au
C: Fe
D: Pb
Ans: B
Exp: Au comes from the Latin word 'Aurum', meaning shining dawn.
---
Q: What organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?
A: Nucleus
B: Ribosome
C: Mitochondria
D: Endoplasmic Reticulum
Ans: C
Exp: Mitochondria generate most of the chemical energy (ATP) needed by the cell.
---
Q: What is the approximate speed of light in a vacuum?
A: 300,000 km/s
B: 150,000 km/s
C: 3,000 km/s
D: 30,000,000 km/s
Ans: A
Exp: The speed of light c is approximately 299,792 km/s (~300,000 km/s).
---
Q: What gas do green plants primarily absorb during photosynthesis?
A: Oxygen
B: Nitrogen
C: Carbon Dioxide
D: Hydrogen
Ans: C
Exp: Plants use carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and release oxygen.
---
Q: Which planet in our solar system is nicknamed the Red Planet?
A: Venus
B: Jupiter
C: Saturn
D: Mars
Ans: D
Exp: Mars has a reddish appearance due to iron oxide (rust) on its surface.
